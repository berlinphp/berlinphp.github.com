## Talk 1: CDNs can be free, cheap or expensive

Content delivery networks used to be technically advanced but expensive solutions used by big corporations only. Nowadays there's players with cheap offers (like Amazon CloudFront) and even free offers (like Google's unlimited free beta of the Pagespeed CDN). This talk includes a live demo of how CloudFront and Pagespeed CDN are configured and a discussion of their respective pros and cons. Participants may want to apply for a free [Pagespeed CDN](https://developers.google.com/speed/pagespeed/service) invitation in advance, b.c. it takes a couple of days to be invited.

by [Timon Schroeter](http://www.php-schulung.de/)
